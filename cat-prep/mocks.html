Mock Tests

15 sectional mocks (5 each in QA, VARC, LRDI) and 5 full-length mocks. Real CAT marking: MCQ +3/−1, TITA +3/0, timed at CAT pace (~1.8 min per question). Your best attempt is saved on this device.

How to attempt: Treat every mock like the real exam — no pausing, no notes, no calculator (CAT allows only the on-screen one). After submitting, spend as long reviewing as you spent attempting: every wrong answer is a syllabus gap; every slow correct answer is a technique gap.
