The CATprep method

Step 1

Learn

Work through the Formula Book, VARC and LRDI pages topic by topic. Ten vocab words and one editorial summary daily, no exceptions.

Step 2

Test

Sectional mocks under a strict timer from week one. Use the AI analyzer on every question you got wrong or solved slowly.

Step 3

Review

One hour of analysis per hour of mock. Log every error, re-solve after three days, and rebuild your attempt strategy from data.
